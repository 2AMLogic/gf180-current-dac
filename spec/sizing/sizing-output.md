## 5. Output impedance for SFDR (first-order model)

Single-ended-equivalent bound (conservative: a differential output
cancels the even-order term, but cell-to-cell mismatch of the two
halves is not modelled). Per-code current I(x) = Ifs*(x - eps*x^2),
x = code/(2^N-1), eps = RL / Z_T, Z_T = Zu / (2^N-1) (all cells on).
HD2 = eps/4, so SFDR = 20*log10(4/eps).

RL = 50 ohm per output, Ifs = 2.0 mA per output.

| Tier | SFDR [dBc] | eps max | Z_T min [kohm] | Zu min per unit [Mohm] | f_tone [MHz] | max code-dependent output C [fF] |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| target | 60 | 0.00400 | 12.50 | 12.79 | 45 | 283 |
| fallback | 50 | 0.01265 | 3.95 | 1.01 | 22 | 1789 |
| stretch | 70 | 0.00126 | 39.53 | 161.87 | 90 | 45 |

The capacitance column replaces Z_T by 1/(j*w*C) for the code-dependent
part of the output capacitance (eps = w*RL*dC). It is a bound on the
part of the output capacitance that varies with code, not on the total.
